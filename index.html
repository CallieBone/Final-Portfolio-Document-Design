Alternative Final

I tried to incorporate a piece of something from each draft and my original to make my final product, which I enjoyed doing. J. Corey. This project went through a lot of changes, and I like those changes because the final is 50% based off my actual real-world resume. The original draft had 2 pages because I used a google doc template, but for someone who is in his field, with not as much work experience, that was not needed. I chose to use my Draft 1 base-layout, a one-pager two column design. I used a darker shade of green, grabbing that color idea from my concept. Instead of making the titles green and the text black, as seen in my original, I made the headings and paragraphs the same color shade of green. This new final also has a photo of the person future employers could possibly be willing to hire. It's just me, but all future employeers should know how you look before the interview, it builds more of a connection. Something my original and Draft 2 excludes that my final doesn't, is a box for his portfolio.
